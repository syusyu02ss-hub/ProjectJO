# 열린 문 근접 배경

- 제작: 내장 image_gen (CLI/API 직접 호출 아님).
- 참고: `game/images/room_cold.png` — 기존 방의 화풍과 문 형태.
- 적용: `game/images/door_threshold.png`, `opened_door` 화면에서 1920×1080으로 표시.
- 원본 방 배경은 보존한다. 인물·손·안내 문구는 넣지 않는다.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Use case: illustration-story. Generate a new 16:9 landscape background for a first-person Korean psychological horror visual novel, ideally 1920x1080. Reference image shows the existing room: match its realistic restrained painted style and the plain dark door (hinges left, metal lever right), NOT its wide camera view. New composition: very CLOSE eye-level view from inside the now unlit room, hesitating immediately before the same door, opened inward just a crack. The near door slab occupies much of the left frame, its lever visible, a narrow opening right of center reveals an almost pitch-black institutional corridor with barely perceptible cool reflected light near the floor. Door frame and wall visible on right. Ceiling lights are off. No bright light streaming through, no daylight. Readable subtle textured shadows rather than total flat black. Door opening, frame and handle should be visible above the bottom 28 percent where the game's dialogue panel will overlay. Emphasize uncertainty and oppressive empty darkness, not overt horror. No people, no hands, no protagonist body, no faces, no silhouettes, no text, signage, UI, logos, gore or monsters.
